Cars Go Vroom

Roll, ride, stop, go

This is a car. A car has wheels. A car carries people. Vroom, vroom, here we go!

Round wheels roll on the road. Roll, roll, roll. The car moves forward.

People sit inside the car. Doors close. Seat belts buckle. Click!

A driver turns the wheel. The car turns too. Left turn, right turn, slow turn.

Some cars use fuel. Some cars plug in and charge. Both kinds need power to go.

Cars come in many shapes. Small cars. Big cars. Speedy racing cars. Beep, beep!

At a stop sign, cars stop. Wait, look, and listen with a grown-up.

Cars can take us to the store, the park, or Grandma's house. Roll, ride, stop, go!
